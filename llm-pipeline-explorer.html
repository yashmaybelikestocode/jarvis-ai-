LLM vs LLP on spelling tokenization

25 commonly misspelled English words, each tokenized spelled correctly and misspelled. LLM subword tokenization learned the correct spellings, so a misspelling breaks it into extra pieces. LLP letter-level tokenization barely notices.

Correct spellingMisspelling
The vocabulary is a small byte-pair encoding trained in your browser on the web examples plus a word list, so counts are smaller than GPT-class tokenizers, but they follow the same pattern.
